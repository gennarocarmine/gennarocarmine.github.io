I had the opportunity to participate in this national conference, representing the diocese of Salerno-Campagna-Acerno along with only 21 other people, 3 of whom were from my own parish. It was a special occasion: for the first time, this type of event was also open to parish leaders and educators, whereas previously it was exclusively for the diocesan level. ([More details on the AC Salerno website](https://www.acsalerno.it/comunicazione/news/buon-natale-5/)).
